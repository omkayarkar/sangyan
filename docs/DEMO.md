# SANGYAN Demo Walkthrough

## Persona: Rahul
Rahul is a 24-year-old first-time investor from a Tier-3 Indian city who receives an investment message on WhatsApp.

### Step 1: Open SANGYAN Web Application
- Rahul opens SANGYAN on his mobile browser.
- He selects his preferred language: **English** or **हिन्दी (Hindi)**.

### Step 2: Share / Upload Suspicious Message
- **Option A (Paste Text):** Rahul pastes:
  `"Earn Guaranteed 100% profit in 7 days! SEBI approved. Transfer ₹5,000 via UPI now to activate daily payout."`
- **Option B (Sample Demo):** Click on *"WhatsApp Guaranteed 100% Return Scam"* chip.

### Step 3: AI Analysis & Output Display
1. **Risk Level:** `High Risk` (Highlighted with Red Badge & Icon)
2. **Risk Score:** `85 / 100` (Labeled: *AI-assisted risk assessment score, NOT a probability that the message is a scam*)
3. **Detected Red Flags:**
   - **Guaranteed Returns:** "This message promises guaranteed or risk-free profits. Market-linked investments always involve risk."
   - **Unverified Regulatory Claim:** "Claims to be SEBI approved. Scammers frequently misuse regulatory names to gain trust."
   - **Request for Money:** "Direct request for upfront deposit or UPI transfer."
4. **Claims Requiring Verification:**
   - SEBI Registration claim -> Verify on official portal `sebi.gov.in`.
5. **Safe Next Steps Checklist:**
   - Do NOT transfer money via UPI.
   - Do NOT share OTPs or bank details.
   - Report to CyberCrime Helpline 1930.
6. **Voice Readout:** Rahul clicks *"Listen Analysis"* to hear the Hindi audio explanation!
