# Security & Privacy Guidelines

## Privacy Guarantees
1. **Zero Storage Policy:** SANGYAN does not save or log uploaded text messages or screenshots in persistent database storage. Analysis is computed in volatile RAM and immediately discarded.
2. **No Credentials Requested:** SANGYAN never asks for passwords, bank account details, credit card numbers, OTPs, Aadhaar numbers, or personal identifying information (PII).
3. **No Third-Party Financial Tracking:** SANGYAN does not require bank integrations, SMS reading permissions, or contact list access.

## Safety & Compliance Guardrails
- SANGYAN strictly complies with Indian Securities Regulations (SEBI Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices).
- SANGYAN explicitly refrains from delivering financial advice, stock recommendations, or trading signals.
